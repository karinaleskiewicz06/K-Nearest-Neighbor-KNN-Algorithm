Remote-first pilot: office supplies ordering & ergonomic chair reimbursement

## Purpose
This page documents the updated, standardized process for handling one-off office supply needs and ergonomic chair reimbursements for employees participating in the Remote-first Pilot program (including on-site pilot week preparation and new-hire onboarding bundles).

This guidance replaces ad-hoc handling captured in internal tickets (e.g., INT-324) and clarifies what is ordered centrally vs. what is reimbursed.

## Scope
Applies to:
- Pilot participants (Engineering, GTM, and other teams explicitly enrolled in the Remote-first Pilot)
- Hiring managers and People Ops supporting pilot new hires
- Office Ops / Procurement and Finance processing pilot-related purchases and reimbursements

Out of scope:
- Standard corporate office refresh projects
- Non-pilot remote-work stipends (see related pages)

## Summary of common pilot requests
The following items are commonly requested for pilot week readiness and onboarding:
- Standing desk converters (temporary loaners) for on-site pilot week
- Assorted cables/adapters kits (USB-C, HDMI, ethernet) to restock shared drawers
- Ergonomic office chair reimbursement (employee-provided receipt) for remote onboarding ergonomics

## What changed since the earlier pilot tickets
- Chair reimbursement cap increased from $350 to $400 for approved pilot new hires (effective 2026-04-01).
- Pre-approval is now required for chair reimbursements (exception: replacement due to documented damage during shipping).
- Reimbursements must be submitted through Expensify (paper forms are no longer accepted).
- Procurement now maintains a preferred vendor list for standing desk converter loaners; direct employee purchase of converters is not reimbursable.

## Policy: allowable items and caps
The following caps apply per employee unless otherwise approved in writing by Finance-legal.

Allowable items (pilot program):
- Standing desk converters: Office Ops orders only; treated as loaner inventory. Not reimbursable.
- Cables/adapters kits: Office Ops orders only; treated as shared office supplies.
- Ergonomic chair: Reimbursable with pre-approval and receipt.

Caps and requirements:
- Ergonomic chair reimbursement cap: $400 (USD) including taxes and shipping.
- Purchase window: within 30 days of employee start date for pilot new hires, unless an exception is approved.
- Receipt must show: vendor name, item description, purchase date, total paid, and payment method.
- Used items are not reimbursable.

## Roles and responsibilities
- Hiring manager: confirms employee eligibility for Remote-first Pilot; initiates chair pre-approval.
- People Ops: confirms the pilot budget allocation and provides the correct budget code.
- Office Ops / Procurement: places orders for converters and shared cables kits; manages loaner inventory and lead times.
- Finance: validates reimbursement submissions, confirms cap compliance, and approves payment timing.

## Procedure A: Ordering standing desk converters (loaner inventory)
1. Hiring manager or pilot coordinator files a request with Office Ops including:
   - Quantity needed
   - Dates needed (pilot week start/end)
   - Delivery location (office or employee shipping address)
2. Office Ops checks existing loaner inventory (see INT-301 reference) and confirms whether additional units are required.
3. If procurement is needed, Office Ops purchases through preferred vendors only.
4. Office Ops tracks assignment and return date. Loaners must be returned within 10 business days after pilot week ends unless extended by Office Ops.

Lead time guidance:
- Typical lead time is 3–5 business days. During peak onboarding periods, plan for 7 business days.

## Procedure B: Ordering cables/adapters kits (shared supplies)
1. Office Ops maintains a standard kit with:
   - USB-C to HDMI
   - USB-C to USB-A
   - HDMI cable
   - Ethernet cable
   - USB-C multiport adapter
2. Requests should specify desired restock quantity (default: 5 kits).
3. Office Ops orders via preferred vendors and stocks the front desk drawer.

## Procedure C: Ergonomic chair reimbursement (pilot new hires)
### Step 1 — Pre-approval (required)
1. Hiring manager submits a pre-approval request to Finance with:
   - Employee name and start date
   - Confirmation the employee is in the Remote-first Pilot
   - Estimated chair cost (must be <= $400)
   - Rationale (ergonomics / onboarding need)
2. Finance responds in writing (email or ticket comment) with approval and the correct expense category/budget code.

### Step 2 — Purchase
- Employee purchases the chair after receiving pre-approval.
- The chair must be new and purchased from a reputable retailer.

### Step 3 — Submission in Expensify
1. Employee submits the expense in Expensify under category: "Remote-first Pilot > Ergonomic Equipment".
2. Attach the itemized receipt.
3. Enter the pre-approval reference (email thread link or ticket key).

### Step 4 — Finance review and payment timing
- Finance verifies cap compliance and receipt validity.
- Processing target: within 5 business days.
- Cutoff for same-pay-cycle reimbursement: submit by end of day Friday before payroll processing week.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from Finance-legal and must include rationale and total cost.
Examples:
- Medical accommodation requiring a specific chair model above the cap (must go through the accommodations process; do not process as a standard pilot exception).
- International shipping constraints that significantly increase shipping cost.

## Validation checklist (for coordinators)
Before marking a request "complete":
- Budget owner approval recorded (People Ops + Finance budget code confirmed)
- Procurement order placed (for converters/cables kits) OR Expensify submitted (for chair reimbursement)
- Receipt attached and legible (for reimbursement)
- Cap confirmed: $400 max (effective 2026-04-01)

## Operational notes
- Direct platform impact is low; however, failure to fulfill these requests creates avoidable friction during pilot week (seat setup delays, onboarding disruptions).
- When in doubt, route questions to Office Ops for ordering and Finance for reimbursements.
