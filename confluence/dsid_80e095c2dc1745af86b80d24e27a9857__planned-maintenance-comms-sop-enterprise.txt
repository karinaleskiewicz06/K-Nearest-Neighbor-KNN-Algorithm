Planned Maintenance Communications SOP (Enterprise)

## Purpose
This SOP defines required customer and internal communications for planned maintenance affecting production workloads, including Redwood Private upgrades.

## When to use
Use this SOP when a planned change has any of:
- Expected downtime
- Risk of degraded performance
- Customer-visible feature behavior changes
- Required customer participation (smoke tests, approvals, access)

## Required communications (minimum)
### 1) Pre-notice (T-5 business days recommended; minimum T-48h for enterprise)
Send a maintenance notice to the customer via the agreed channel (usually email + shared Slack Connect if available). Include:
- Date/time window in customers timezone
- Expected impact (downtime vs degraded; which routes/models)
- Rollback posture (we will rollback if KPIs exceed thresholds)
- Required customer participation (who needs to be present)
- Redwood escalation contact(s)

### 2) Internal heads-up (T-48h)
Post a short note in #eng-oncall and #customer-success with:
- Customer name
- Window time
- Change owner
- Link to change record
- Whether a security sign-off is required

### 3) Window start message (T=0)
Send a starting now message to the customer and internal channels. Confirm the backout plan exists and the next update time.

### 4) Progress updates
- Default cadence: every 30 minutes during the window
- Immediate updates for any rollback or risk escalation

### 5) All-clear (post-window)
Send an all-clear message when the verification window is complete. Include:
- Completed work summary
- Any follow-ups
- Post-change monitoring plan (e.g., 24h heightened watch)

## Status page guidance
- Hosted API: use public status page events when broad customer impact is possible.
- Private: do not post customer-specific maintenance on the public status page. Use direct customer comms unless contractually required otherwise.

## Templates
### Maintenance notice (customer)
Subject: Planned maintenance for your Redwood Private deployment
Body: We plan to perform maintenance on <DATE/TIME> for <DURATION>. Expected impact: <IMPACT>. Your participation needed: <WHO/WHAT>. Rollback criteria: <THRESHOLDS>.

### All-clear
Subject: Maintenance complete
Body: Maintenance completed at <TIME>. We verified <SMOKE TESTS>. Monitoring: <WHAT/WHO>.

## Approval
Customer Success must approve customer-facing maintenance notices for enterprise accounts.
