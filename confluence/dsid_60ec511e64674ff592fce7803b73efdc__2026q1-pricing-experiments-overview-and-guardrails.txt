2026 Q1 Pricing Experiments — Overview, Guardrails, and Measurement Standards

## Purpose
This page catalogs Redwood Inference’s **Q1 2026 pricing & packaging experiments** for the Hosted API (and limited Dedicated add-ons), including:
- Experiment variants and hypotheses
- Eligibility/cohort definitions
- Success metrics and stop criteria
- Guardrails to prevent **revenue leakage**, **customer harm**, and **RevRec/Legal risk**

This doc is the **source of truth** for which experiments are allowed to run in Q1, the required measurement standards, and the approvals needed to change any pricing surface.

> Important: Because Q1 also includes metering hardening work, experiment readouts must explicitly segment **pre-meter-fix vs post-meter-fix** periods (see “Metering integrity requirements”).

---

## Q1 principles (non-negotiables)
1) **No silent changes to billing semantics.** Any change that could affect what a customer is billed for requires Finance + Legal review and must be documented (including a shadow-billing compare period where applicable).
2) **Customer trust beats short-term conversion.** If an experiment increases support tickets, disputes, or perceived unfairness in usage definitions, it must be stopped even if conversion improves.
3) **Guardrails are enforced by default.** If instrumentation or safeguards are missing, the experiment does not launch.
4) **One pricing change at a time per account.** Avoid overlapping experiments for the same workspace/account unless explicitly designed as a factorial test.

---

## Definitions (used across all experiments)
### Core outcomes
- **Activation**: account generates successful billed usage on Hosted API within 7 days of signup AND returns for at least 2 additional active days.
- **Paid conversion (self-serve)**: account attaches payment method and exceeds free tier OR upgrades to a paid plan.
- **Expansion (sales-led)**: committed spend / dedicated capacity increases vs baseline.
- **Retention**: D30 active usage for self-serve; logo retention for sales-led.

### Unit economics
- **Gross margin (GM)** is evaluated using FP&A’s model inputs and telemetry (tokens, requests, cache-hit attribution, and infra cost allocation). GM must be reviewed with FP&A when meter definitions change.

### Customer harm signals
- Support ticket rate per 1k active accounts (Billing/Metering tagged)
- Invoice dispute rate and credit/refund requests
- “Surprise bill” indicators: large day-over-day spend increases not explained by usage

---

## Metering integrity requirements (applies to all experiments)
Because pricing tests depend on usage data quality, the following must be true before any experiment is considered “valid”:
- Meter definitions are pinned to the published spec (see Billing Meter Spec page).
- The analysis plan must tag results as **pre/post meter fixes** if any metering changes overlap.
- For meter-impacting engineering changes, run **shadow billing compare** for at least one full weekly billing cycle prior to affecting invoices.
- All experiment dashboards must include: data freshness, duplicate event rate, null dimensions, and drift vs billing ledger.

If any of the following occur, the experiment is **paused** until resolved:
- Material drift vs billing ledger (as defined by Finance thresholds)
- Missing region/model dimensions required for pricing attribution
- Suspected double-counting on retries/fallback paths

## EXP-26Q1-01 — Self-serve Free Tier: “Starter Credits + Clear Overage”
**Stage:** Running (limited rollout)

**Goal:** Improve signup-to-activation and reduce early churn without increasing billing disputes.

**Hypothesis:** Users activate more reliably when (a) the free tier is framed as credits/included usage with (b) an explicit overage policy and spend guardrails.

**Eligibility / cohort:**
- New self-serve signups only
- Exclusions: accounts created via sales-assisted flow; internal/test; flagged fraud risk
- Randomization: 50/50 at workspace creation; persistent assignment

**Variants:**
- Control (A): current free tier messaging + existing default spend behavior
- Variant (B): “Starter credits” framing + explicit overage messaging + default spend guardrail prompt

**Primary success metrics:**
- Activation rate (D7)
- Paid conversion within 30 days (self-serve)

**Secondary metrics:**
- D30 retention
- Support tickets tagged Billing/Metering per 1k actives
- Payment method attach rate

**Stop criteria (hard):**
- +25% or more increase in billing/metering support tickets vs control for 3 consecutive days
- Statistically meaningful increase in dispute/credit requests (Finance review)
- Any evidence of ambiguous or misleading overage copy (Legal review)

**Guardrails / required instrumentation:**
- “Estimated spend” UI must be visible prior to first overage event
- Usage export must show billed vs raw usage fields (where available)
- Cohort tag must be present in analytics events and CRM properties

**Notes:**
- Any change to spend guardrail behavior must be reviewed by Finance to avoid revenue leakage via unintended caps.

## EXP-26Q1-02 — Packaging: Bundled Included Tokens for Pro Tier (Self-Serve)
**Stage:** Planned (pending meter-fix segmentation plan)

**Goal:** Increase ARPU and predictability for high-frequency self-serve accounts without lowering GM.

**Hypothesis:** A predictable monthly bundle (included tokens) increases willingness to pay vs pure overage, especially for customers with stable usage.

**Eligibility / cohort:**
- Self-serve accounts with >= 2 weeks of consistent usage OR high intent (multiple team members + sustained daily usage)
- Exclusions: customers currently in a contract with custom pricing; accounts with open billing disputes

**Variants:**
- Control (A): usage-only pricing baseline
- Variant (B): monthly subscription fee that includes a token allowance + standard overage beyond allowance

**Primary success metrics:**
- Net revenue per active account (ARPA) at D30 and D60
- Gross margin delta vs control (FP&A model)

**Secondary metrics:**
- Retention (D60)
- Plan downgrade/cancel rate
- Support ticket rate and “pricing confusion” qualitative feedback

**Stop criteria (hard):**
- GM declines beyond Finance-approved threshold for 2 consecutive weekly reads
- Material increase in cancellation/downgrade rate

**Guardrails:**
- No back-billing: bundles apply only forward from upgrade date
- Subscription invoices must show included usage vs overage clearly (RevRec review)
- Ensure metering parity across OpenAI-compatible and native endpoints prior to launch

## EXP-26Q1-03 — Overage Rounding & Minimum Billable Units (Hosted API)
**Stage:** Paused (meter-definition dependency)

**Goal:** Reduce perceived “nickel-and-diming” while preserving revenue integrity.

**Hypothesis:** Reducing rounding surprises improves trust and reduces disputes, with minimal revenue impact.

**Eligibility / cohort:**
- All self-serve paid accounts (excluding enterprise contracts)
- Segmented analysis by model family and endpoint type

**Variants:**
- Control (A): existing rounding/minimum units
- Variant (B): revised rounding rules (customer-friendly) applied consistently across endpoints

**Primary success metrics:**
- Dispute rate and billing-related support tickets

**Secondary metrics:**
- Revenue impact vs baseline
- Changes in usage behavior (request size distribution)

**Stop criteria (hard):**
- Any rounding rule creates ambiguous interpretation in contract definitions (Legal veto)
- Revenue leakage beyond Finance-approved materiality threshold

**Guardrails:**
- Must align exactly with the Billing Meter Spec and be mirrored in usage exports
- Requires shadow billing compare for at least 2 weeks before enforcement

## EXP-26Q1-04 — Dedicated Add-on: Burst Capacity Pricing (Sales-led)
**Stage:** Running (sales-assisted, narrow set)

**Goal:** Improve expansion/upsell for Dedicated customers needing occasional bursts, without creating unpredictable invoices.

**Hypothesis:** A clearly defined burst add-on (with caps and notifications) increases expansion while preventing surprise spend.

**Eligibility / cohort:**
- Existing Dedicated customers with documented burst events (capacity saturation) in prior 60 days
- Requires signed addendum / order form language for burst usage definition

**Variants:**
- Control (A): manual burst approval + ad hoc invoicing
- Variant (B): standardized burst add-on with pre-set rates, caps, and alerting

**Primary success metrics:**
- Expansion revenue and renewal confidence
- Reduction in churn risk flagged by CSM

**Secondary metrics:**
- Support escalations related to “unexpected burst charges”
- Operational load (approvals, exceptions)

**Stop criteria (hard):**
- Any customer reports charges without prior notification or exceeding agreed caps
- Any mismatch between Dedicated GPU-seconds meter and invoiced burst line item

**Guardrails:**
- Requires Dedicated GPU-seconds meter v2 (or shadow-compare parity) for participating pools
- Notifications: email + Console banner when burst begins and at 50%/80% of cap

## EXP-26Q1-05 — Credits & Discounts: Guardrailed Promotions for Self-Serve
**Stage:** Planned (RevOps + Finance approval)

**Goal:** Improve conversion for high-intent self-serve without creating revrec complexity or abuse.

**Hypothesis:** Limited-time promotional credits convert evaluators into paid users when tied to product activation milestones.

**Eligibility / cohort:**
- New self-serve accounts only
- Exclusions: suspicious usage patterns; accounts with high refund risk; existing paid accounts

**Variants:**
- Control (A): standard free tier only
- Variant (B): promotional credit issued after activation milestone

**Primary success metrics:**
- Paid conversion at D30
- Net revenue at D60 (post-credit)

**Secondary metrics:**
- Abuse rate (multiple accounts, unusual IP/device patterns)
- Credit-related support tickets

**Stop criteria (hard):**
- Evidence of systematic credit abuse beyond acceptable threshold
- RevRec flags material increase in manual adjustments

**Guardrails:**
- Credits must have explicit expiry and applicability rules
- Credits must apply deterministically to billed meters and be reflected in invoice preview
- Any exception requires the Discounting & Credits approval matrix

## Standard measurement plan (required)
Each experiment must ship with:
1) **Experiment ID** surfaced in:
   - Analytics events
   - HubSpot/CRM properties (where relevant)
   - Billing preview exports (internal)
2) **Dashboards** with:
   - Topline conversion/retention
   - Unit economics (GM proxy; tokens/request mix)
   - Customer harm signals
   - Meter sanity tiles (freshness, duplicates, drift)
3) **Readout cadence**
   - Weekly cross-functional review (Product + Finance + Eng)
   - Midpoint decision (continue/stop/modify) documented here

---

## Guardrails checklist (launch gate)
An experiment cannot launch until all items are TRUE:
- [ ] Eligibility rules documented and implemented (with exclusions)
- [ ] Primary metric, secondary metrics, and stop criteria defined
- [ ] Legal-reviewed customer-facing copy (if any)
- [ ] Finance-reviewed expected revenue/GM impact band
- [ ] Metering sanity checks passing for affected endpoints/regions/models
- [ ] Backout plan exists (flag kill switch + comms plan)
- [ ] Support is briefed with an FAQ + escalation path

---

## Customer communications policy
- Do not represent experiments as permanent pricing.
- If customers ask about changes, provide truthful high-level context and direct them to the current plan terms.
- Any customer-facing statement about tokens, retries, caching, or streaming must match the Legal-approved usage definitions.
