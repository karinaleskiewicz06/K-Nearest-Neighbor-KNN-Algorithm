LatticeBridge Support — QBR Account Brief (2026 Q1)

LatticeBridge Support is evaluating Redwood Dedicated to replace their in-house retrieval + Lambda-based reranker in order to increase top-5 answer precision and reduce per-query infrastructure cost for ticket deflection and agent assist. In Q1, the team completed a Dedicated POC on a 1M-document subset and validated latency targets for their NA interactive path, with measurable top-5 precision lift over their current Elastic + bespoke reranker baseline. Primary open items are finalizing VPC peering architecture approval, confirming data residency language, and aligning on a commercial model for burst traffic (light deflection checks) vs sustained full rerank traffic.
