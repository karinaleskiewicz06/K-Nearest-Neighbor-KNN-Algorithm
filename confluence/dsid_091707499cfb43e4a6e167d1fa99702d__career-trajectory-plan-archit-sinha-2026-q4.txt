Career trajectory plan (Archit Sinha) — 2026 Q4 (Platform Infra)

## Overview
This page consolidates the agreed career trajectory direction and a concrete Q4 2026 execution plan for Archit Sinha (IC, Platform Infra). It is intended to be a durable reference for Archit, the Platform team, and partner leads when prioritizing work and assessing growth.

The focus of Q4 is a structured “tech lead trial” that is IC-first (no people management expectations), with explicit success criteria and follow-up checkpoints.

## Current role snapshot (as of 2026-10-15)
- Role: Platform Infra IC (serving/runtime performance + CI reliability)
- Strengths observed: deep implementation ownership, performance debugging, pragmatic scoping, strong follow-through on operational issues
- Growth edge for TL track: cross-team alignment, writing/communicating design intent early, driving a milestone plan with partner commitments

## Career direction
### Near-term direction (Q4 2026)
Archit is pursuing a Tech Lead (TL) trajectory experiment, centered on owning a small cross-team performance initiative (KV cache) with clear milestones, partner alignment, and visible outcomes.

### What “TL” means in this context
- Ownership of a problem area end-to-end: goals → design → execution plan → delivery → measurement
- Driving alignment across partners (Runtime, Model Efficiency, SRE/Infra)
- Being the single threaded owner for status, risks, and decision-making
- Not a requirement: direct reports or formal people management

## Q4 2026 goals and scope
### Primary initiative: KV cache improvements spike → productionization plan
Archit will lead a time-boxed effort to validate and recommend KV-cache improvements, including an initial prototype and a productionization plan.

**Timebox**
- Spike: 4 weeks (target start: 2026-10-07)
- Outcome checkpoint: 2026-11-06
- If successful: draft production plan for Q1 2027 (no commitment implied until review)

**Deliverables (must-haves)**
1) Design/RFC (1–3 pages)
   - Problem statement, baseline metrics, constraints
   - Options considered and tradeoffs
   - Rollout/guardrail plan and how we will measure success
2) Prototype implementation and benchmark harness updates
3) Results summary with recommendation
   - “Proceed” / “Do not proceed” / “Proceed with constraints”
   - Expected impact and operational risks

### Secondary initiative: CI reliability improvements (unblocking critical PR flow)
Archit will continue to contribute to CI reliability, but the expectation is to shift from “hero debugging” to “systematic fix + clear ownership.”

**Scope in Q4**
- Close out remaining flake sources on staging e2e pipeline (see INF-1203)
- Implement or formalize a gating strategy (quarantine rules + owner rotation)

## Success criteria (measurable)
### KV cache initiative success criteria
A successful Q4 outcome is meeting *all* of the following:
- RFC is reviewed by at least two partner teams (Runtime + one of Model Efficiency/SRE) and feedback is incorporated.
- Prototype demonstrates one of:
  - >= 12% throughput improvement on the agreed benchmark suite, OR
  - >= 8% token-cost reduction on the agreed end-to-end workload,
  with methodology documented and reproducible.
- Clear rollout and guardrail plan is proposed (metrics + canary + rollback).

### TL skill signals we are explicitly looking for
- Proactive alignment: partner review scheduled before implementation is “done”
- Crisp scoping: milestone plan with what is in/out for the timebox
- Decision hygiene: tradeoffs documented; decisions communicated broadly
- Operational awareness: performance improvements accompanied by stability and observability considerations

## Known blockers / risks and mitigations
### CI flakiness history and current state
- GH-PR-4721 (runtime performance changes) is **merged** as of late August; the original CI instability that blocked it has been reduced but not eliminated.
- INF-1203 (flaky staging e2e) remains the top reliability risk for Q4 velocity.

**Mitigation plan**
1) Establish a “flake triage” rotation owner for the staging e2e suite (weekly) and define escalation thresholds.
2) Quarantine policy: tests that fail >= 2 times/week without recent code change are moved to quarantine within 48 hours, with an owner and a ticket.
3) Dedicated runner usage is allowed for performance-sensitive branches during the KV cache spike if it reduces noise; long-term solution remains stabilizing the shared fleet.
