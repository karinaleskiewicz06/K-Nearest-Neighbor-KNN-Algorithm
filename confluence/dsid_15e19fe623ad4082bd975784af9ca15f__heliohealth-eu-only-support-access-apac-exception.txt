Data residency exception record: HelioHealth (EU-only request with APAC support ops access)

h2. Summary
HelioHealth requested EU-only processing/storage for patient-adjacent text. Redwood Inference approved an exception permitting limited out-of-region *support access* from APAC-based Redwood Support engineers to view *audit logs and operational metadata* for troubleshooting, while keeping serving and data storage in EU.

h2. Customer requirement
* EU-only data residency for serving and storage.
* Customer allows Redwood support access for break-glass troubleshooting but requested that access be constrained and logged.

h2. Approved exception
* Exception type: Out-of-region *support access* to operational metadata
* Affected regions: eu-west (serving/storage), apac (support personnel location)
* Scope:
  * Support personnel in APAC may access Console audit logs and request metadata (request ids, timestamps, model name, status codes) for the HelioHealth tenant.
  * Prompts/responses are not stored; payload logging remains disabled.
* Duration: 2024-10-05 through 2025-04-30
* Controls:
  * Break-glass role required; access is time-limited and recorded in audit logs.
  * Customer receives monthly access report on request.
