Enterprise Security Enablement Kit (Index)

## Purpose
This page is the single entry point for Redwood Inference’s **Enterprise Security Enablement Kit**. It provides:
- The **current customer-facing Security FAQ** (Hosted / Dedicated / Private)
- Approved **compliance/security one-pagers** for sharing in deal cycles
- The **standard RFP response library** (approved language + approval rules)
- The **SOC 2 / evidence request process** (NDA + SLAs)
- Internal owners, escalation paths, and “what we do / don’t say” guardrails

**Audience:** Sales, Solutions Engineering, Customer Success (Enterprise), Security/GRC, and Partner teams.

---

## What you can send to customers (default package)
Use this checklist to keep deal cycles moving without over-sharing restricted artifacts:

1) **Send the Security FAQ (public-to-customer, no NDA required)**
- Confluence: `sales-enablement/security-faq/redwood-enterprise-security-faq-2026.json`
- Guidance: Use the FAQ as the canonical source for questionnaire answers unless the customer has a required format (then use the RFP library language).

2) **Send relevant one-pagers (customer-facing summaries)**
- Data Residency + Retention (one-pager)
- Encryption + KMS (one-pager)
- SOC 2 posture overview (one-pager; the report itself is NDA-gated)
- ISO/27001 alignment (one-pager; alignment language only—do not imply certification)

3) **If they request SOC 2 report / pentest letter / policy artifacts → follow the Evidence Request Process**
- Do not attach restricted artifacts directly from personal email/Drive.

## SLAs and expected turnaround (internal)
**Security FAQ / standard answer questions**
- Owner: Solutions Engineering (first line), Security Engineering (backup)
- SLA: 1–2 business days for “is this supported / how do we phrase it?”

**Evidence requests (SOC 2 report, restricted artifacts, customer security portals)**
- Owner: GRC
- SLA (standard): 3 business days after NDA is in place and request is complete
- SLA (expedite): 1 business day with executive sponsor approval (CISO or GTM leadership) and a confirmed deal deadline

**RFP response deviations from approved language**
- Owner: Legal + Security/GRC (depending on section)
- SLA: Depends on scope; plan 2–5 business days. Escalate early if a hard RFP deadline exists.

## Quick reminders (common pitfalls)
- **ISO:** Use “alignment” language only unless GRC explicitly confirms a current certification status for a specific customer statement. Default to the approved wording in “What we do / don’t say.”
- **Data residency:** Avoid absolutes (“all data stays in-region”) unless the specific deployment mode and controls match the customer’s requirement. Use the data residency + retention one-pager and the approved EU caveats.
- **“No training on customer data”:** Use the precise approved definition in the FAQ/claims guidance (avoid ambiguous phrasing like “never stored anywhere”).
- **Pen tests / reports:** Do not promise to share full pentest reports as a default. Route via evidence request process and approved artifact list.
