Runtime incident customer communications (status page + direct customer updates)

h1. Purpose
This page describes the required communications for Serving Runtime incidents, including regressions that lead to an emergency rollback.

h1. Who owns comms
* Comms Lead: typically Customer Success oncall (or Support lead after-hours).
* IC approves external messaging for SEV1/SEV2.
* For Dedicated premium SLA tenants, Account Owner must be looped (sales/CS) even during SEV1.

h1. When to post to the public status page
* SEV1: post within 15 minutes of declaration if there is confirmed customer impact in Hosted or multiple Dedicated tenants.
* SEV2: post if impact is user-visible and expected to last > 30 minutes, or if many customers are likely to notice.
* Never post for canary-only SEV3 issues unless instructed by IC.

h1. Required content (status page)
Include:
* What customers observe (symptoms)
* Affected product surface (Hosted API, Dedicated clusters)
* Regions (if known)
* Current mitigation (e.g., paused rollout, rolling back runtime)
* Next update time

h1. Dedicated customer notifications
For Dedicated incidents, in addition to status page (if applicable):
* Email the impacted tenant distribution list and open a support ticket on their behalf (if not already open).
* If the customer has an on-call bridge procedure, ask them to join once we have an ETA or a mitigation plan.

h1. Templates

## Initial status page post (SEV1)
"We are investigating elevated error rates and increased latency affecting the Redwood Inference API in some regions. We have paused the current Serving Runtime rollout and are preparing mitigations. Next update in 30 minutes."

## Update when rollback begins
"We identified a regression in a recent Serving Runtime release and have started an emergency rollback. We are monitoring recovery metrics across regions and will provide an update within 30 minutes."

## Resolved
"The issue has been mitigated and service metrics have returned to normal following a rollback. We will publish a post-incident review."

h1. Approval checklist
Before sending customer-facing comms:
* IC has reviewed the wording
* Do not speculate root cause
* Do not share internal release tags or cluster names unless customer-specific and approved
* If security is suspected, follow the security incident process instead
