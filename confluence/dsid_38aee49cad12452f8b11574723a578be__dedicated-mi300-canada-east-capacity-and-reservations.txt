Dedicated MI300 in Canada East (ca-central-1): Capacity, Reservations, and Routing

## Overview
We operate a Dedicated MI300 pool in Canada East (region: ca-central-1). The pool supports:
- Reserved (Dedicated) capacity for committed customers and approved internal workloads.
- Best-effort burst capacity for eligible accounts when headroom is available.
- Cross-region failover to alternate GPU fleets when the MI300 pool is depleted.

This page replaces the initial soft-launch announcement with current capacity, reservation workflow, routing behavior, and operational notes.

## Current capacity (as of 2025-02-03)
**Dedicated reserved pool:** 16 MI300 instances (ca-central-1)
**Burst pool (best-effort):** up to +8 MI300 instances when available
**Notes:**
- Burst capacity is preemptible and may be reclaimed during peak demand.
- Capacity may be temporarily reduced during host maintenance windows.

## Eligibility and rollout status
- **Reserved capacity:** Available to committed customers and approved internal teams.
- **Burst capacity:** Available to accounts flagged as burst-eligible by Sales Ops / Capacity Ops.
- **Public quota:** Limited GA is now enabled for qualified self-serve customers (initially small quotas). Larger quotas still require approval.

## Pricing and commercial notes
- Reserved MI300 capacity follows the standard MI300 tier rate.
- Committed-use discounts are handled via Sales (deal desk approval required).
- Cross-region failover may change unit pricing depending on the fallback fleet.

## Reservation workflow
### Console
Use the Dedicated reservations page:
https://console.redwood.ai/dedicated/reservations

### CLI
Example (monthly term):
redwood dedicated reserve --region ca-central-1 --model mi300 --count 1 --term monthly

Approval notes:
- Reservations are subject to capacity approval.
- If you need a short-term evaluation slot, request a temporary dev reservation from Capacity Ops.

### Required configuration for reserved workloads
1) **Pin model versions** when running on reserved MI300 to avoid cross-variant runtime issues during rolling upgrades.
2) **Declare quantization format** (fp16/bf16/int8/int4) in your deployment config.
3) **Set a routing policy** if you require explicit fallback behavior (see below).

## Routing and fallback behavior
### Default behavior
If the ca-central-1 MI300 Dedicated pool is depleted, the default policy will fail over to:
- **l40s-us-east** (cross-region) for general availability workloads.

### Optional fallbacks
Teams can request or configure additional fallbacks depending on workload requirements:
- **h100-us-east** for stricter latency/throughput targets (subject to quota and higher cost).

### How to change routing
- Use a custom routing policy in the console, or
- File a support ticket with:
  - service name / deployment ID
  - desired primary region/fleet
  - ordered fallback list
  - max acceptable latency and cost constraints

## Latency / reliability targets
- **Target SLO (typical generation workloads):** p99 < 120ms (regional) under standard load patterns.
- For more stringent p99 targets, tag your rollout and coordinate with on-call so we can prioritize routing and triage.

Monitoring guidance:
- Track p50/p95/p99 by region and fleet.
- Alert on sustained p99 regression > 15% over 30 minutes after a deployment change.

## Runtime and compatibility notes
- MI300 runtime: ROCm 6.2 (kernel + driver stack managed by Infra).
- Quantized checkpoints (int8/int4): validate in staging before production rollout.
- Known caveat: some older fused ops / layernorm kernels can behave slightly differently across driver/runtime updates; report regressions to eng-ml with:
  - model name + version
  - quantization format
  - minimal repro prompt set
  - observed vs expected metrics

## Policy notes
1) Burst capacity is best-effort and may be reclaimed with limited notice during peak.
2) Reserved capacity customers must pin model versions.
3) Private deployments require separate onboarding; this pool is hosted Dedicated only.

## Operational ownership and escalation
- Primary owner: Capacity Ops (Eng-Infra)
- On-call: Infra On-Call (route via standard incident channel)

Escalate immediately if:
- You observe repeated reservation failures for approved accounts.
- Pool utilization is at/near saturation for > 2 hours with customer impact.
- Cross-region failover triggers unexpectedly for reserved workloads.
