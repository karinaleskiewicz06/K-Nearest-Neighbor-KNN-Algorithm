Month-End Close: Usage Reconciliation Checklist (Hosted + Dedicated)

## Purpose
This checklist is the standard month-end close procedure for reconciling usage-based revenue inputs (Hosted API + Dedicated) across:
1) telemetry usage events (engineering source)
2) billing ledger (invoiceable source)
3) customer-facing usage exports/API (customer source)

It is designed for audit readiness: repeatable steps, defined thresholds, documented exceptions, and explicit sign-offs.

**Scope:**
- Hosted API: chat/text generation, embeddings, reranking (tokens/requests; embeddings batch accounting)
- Dedicated: GPU-seconds (v1/v2 as applicable during transition), reserved commits vs burst usage
- Adjustments: credits/refunds/waivers applied to invoices (validation only; approvals handled in separate finance workflow)

**Out of scope:** Pricing experiment analysis (handled by Product Ops) except where meter changes overlap with the close window.

## Month-end timing and freeze window
**Standard close cadence:**
- **T-3 business days:** pre-close health checks; identify high-risk accounts and pipeline issues.
- **T-1 business day:** confirm “usage freeze window” and expected late-arriving event cutoff.
- **T (month-end 23:59:59 UTC):** month closes.
- **T+1 to T+3:** primary reconciliation and exception triage.
- **T+4 to T+5:** final approvals, invoice preview validation, archive evidence.

**Usage freeze window (default):**
- Telemetry ingestion remains on, but the **RevRec “close snapshot”** is taken at **T+2 12:00 UTC**.
- Events arriving after that time are treated as **late events** and require exception handling (see below).
- Backfills affecting the closed period require Finance approval and must be logged with an audit trail.

## Source-of-truth definitions (use consistently)
### Hosted API
- **Usage events:** `usage_events_hosted_api` (token-level aggregated per request)
- **Invoiceable ledger:** `billing_ledger_line_items` (month partition)
- **Customer usage:** Usage API/export backed by `customer_usage_rollups` (should match ledger at aggregate level)

### Dedicated
- **Usage events:** `usage_events_dedicated_gpu_seconds` (per pool/account/time window)
- **Invoiceable ledger:** `billing_ledger_line_items` (Dedicated SKU lines)
- **Customer usage:** Dedicated usage export (`customer_usage_rollups_dedicated`)

**Important:** If meter logic changes during the month (e.g., token counting alignment, retry dedupe, GPU-seconds v2 shadow compare), segment analysis **pre-change vs post-change** for interpretability. For invoicing and RevRec, the ledger definition for the period remains authoritative unless Finance approves a change.

## Reconciliation thresholds (pass/fail criteria)
These thresholds determine whether we can proceed to invoice preview and close, or whether we must open an exception and/or block close.

### A) Data freshness and completeness (blocking)
1. **Freshness SLO (usage events → rollups):**
   - Hosted API rollups updated within **<= 6 hours** of event time for the final 72 hours of the month.
   - Dedicated rollups updated within **<= 12 hours** of event time for the final 72 hours of the month.
2. **Completeness:**
   - Missing partitions/days in month: **0** allowed.
   - Null critical dimensions rate (account_id, product, region, model, timestamp bucket): **<= 0.05%**.
   - Duplicate event rate (idempotency collisions/duplicates): **<= 0.10%** of events.

### B) Usage-to-ledger drift (investigate; may block)
Compute drift at multiple cuts (overall, by product, by top accounts, by region/model).

**Hosted API drift thresholds (tokens/requests):**
- Overall month drift (usage events aggregate vs billing ledger): **<= 0.20%** tokens and **<= 0.10%** revenue equivalent.
- Top 20 accounts drift (per account): **<= 0.50%** tokens or **<= $2,500** (whichever is greater).
- Any single account-day drift: **<= 1.00%** tokens or **<= $1,000**.

**Dedicated drift thresholds (GPU-seconds):**
- Overall month drift: **<= 0.50%** GPU-seconds or **<= $5,000**.
- Per pool/account drift: **<= 1.00%** GPU-seconds.

### C) Customer usage export parity (investigate; not always blocking)
- Monthly totals in customer-facing usage exports should match billing ledger within **<= 0.10%** by SKU.
- If mismatch exists due to known release timing (e.g., new fields, rounding presentation), document clearly and ensure customer-facing totals used for invoice match the invoice.

## Checklist (execute in order)
### 1) Pre-close health checks (T-3 to T-1)
1. Confirm no active P0/P1 incidents for:
   - usage event ingestion
   - rollup jobs
   - billing ledger generation
   - usage export/API
2. Review Meter Sanity Dashboard:
   - freshness panels
   - duplicate rate
   - null-rate for critical dimensions
   - meter drift vs ledger (last 7 days)
3. Identify “high-risk” conditions and open tracking tickets as needed:
   - ongoing backfills
   - recent PRs affecting metering logic
   - known missing dimensions (e.g., region)
   - elevated retries/fallback traffic

**Evidence to save:** screenshot links or exported dashboard snapshots for the last 7 days + incident references.

---

### 2) Data completeness checks (T+1)
Run and record results for the closed month:
1. Partition completeness (all days present) for each required table.
2. Critical dimension null checks (account_id, product, region, model, meter_version, pricing_plan_id where applicable).
3. Duplicate/idempotency checks:
   - duplicate usage_event_id rate
   - multiple ledger line items for the same request_id/idempotency_key (Hosted)
4. Late-arriving events volume (events with event_time in closed month arriving after snapshot time):
   - quantify by day and by top accounts

**Pass criteria:** Meets thresholds in section “Reconciliation thresholds (A)”.

**If fail:** Open a **Month-End Meter Exception** (see section 6) and page/engage Telemetry DRI.

---

### 3) Hosted API reconciliation (T+1 to T+2)
Perform reconciliation at three levels:

**Level 1 — Total month:**
- Compare total billed tokens, requests, and revenue equivalent between:
  - usage events aggregate
  - billing ledger
  - customer usage export/API

**Level 2 — By SKU / endpoint:**
- Chat/text generation: prompt_tokens, completion_tokens, billed_tokens
- Embeddings: inputs, billed units, batching accounting
- Rerank: requests, billed units

**Level 3 — Segmented drill-downs:**
- By account (top 20 by spend)
- By region and model
- By cache_hit attribution (where available) and retry/fallback indicators

**Required notes:**
- Confirm token counting semantics are consistent with the Hosted API meter spec for this month.
- If a meter change occurred mid-month (e.g., retry dedupe fix), explicitly segment the analysis and document expected deltas.

**Pass criteria:** Meets thresholds in section “Reconciliation thresholds (B/C)”.

---

### 4) Dedicated reconciliation (T+1 to T+2)
1. Validate each Dedicated pool has:
   - continuous coverage across the month
   - expected autoscaling/idle accounting behavior (per definition)
2. Compare:
   - total GPU-seconds vs billing ledger
   - reserved commit vs burst usage (if applicable)
   - per pool/account totals and drift
3. If GPU-seconds v2 shadow compare is running:
   - save shadow compare summary
   - confirm which meter version is invoice-affecting for the closed month

**Pass criteria:** Meets thresholds in section “Reconciliation thresholds (B/C)”.

---

### 5) Credits, adjustments, and invoice preview validation (T+2 to T+4)
1. Validate credits/adjustments applied in the ledger match approved finance records for the month.
2. For any customer with open billing dispute tickets:
   - confirm whether credits are scheduled this invoice vs next cycle
   - confirm customer-facing usage exports align with what will be invoiced
3. Run invoice preview checks (top accounts + any accounts with exceptions):
   - line item totals reconcile to ledger totals
   - usage units align to expected meters (tokens/requests/GPU-seconds)
   - plan/price mapping is correct (no missing pricing dimensions)

**Evidence to save:** approval references (ticket IDs/email), invoice preview export, and ledger snapshot identifier.

## Exception handling (how to proceed when thresholds fail)
### Exception severity
**SEV-A (Blocking / must resolve or obtain exec sign-off):**
- missing partitions/days
- freshness/completeness thresholds breached in a way that could materially impact invoices
- overall month drift above threshold for Hosted or Dedicated

**SEV-B (Non-blocking with documented rationale):**
- isolated account drift beyond threshold with understood root cause and contained impact
- customer usage export parity mismatch caused by presentation/rounding or known rollout timing (not invoice-affecting)

**SEV-C (Monitor):**
- small anomalies below thresholds but worth tracking (e.g., slight increase in duplicate rate)

### Required steps for any exception
1. Open a tracking ticket: **“Month-End Meter Exception: YYYY-MM close”** and assign DRIs (Finance + Eng).
2. Record:
   - scope (tables, dates, products, impacted accounts)
   - measured impact (units and $)
   - root cause hypothesis
   - mitigation (rerun job, backfill, shadow compare, manual adjustment)
3. Decide outcome:
   - **Forward fix only** (preferred), no retroactive invoice changes
   - **Ledger adjustment** requires Finance approval; Legal review if contract/dispute implications
4. Obtain sign-off:
   - SEV-A: CFO delegate (Laura Bennett) + RevRec owner
   - SEV-B: RevRec owner + Eng DRI

## Documentation and audit readiness requirements
For each month-end close, store evidence in the Finance/Legal restricted close folder and link from the close memo:

**Minimum evidence bundle:**
- Data completeness results (queries or exported CSV) and timestamps
- Drift reconciliation summary (Hosted + Dedicated) including segmentation notes
- List of exceptions with severity, impact, and approvals
- Snapshot identifiers / job run IDs for the ledger and rollups used
- Invoice preview validation notes for top accounts
- Any backfill plan and confirmation of whether it affected the closed month

**Retention:** Follow the “Metering Data Retention and Access Controls” policy. Ensure all artifacts are stored in approved systems with access logging.
