Sev-1 Communication Requirements (Status Page + Customer Notifications)

h2. Goal
Ensure consistent, timely communication during Sev-1 incidents across Hosted and Dedicated customers. This page defines *what must be communicated*, *who does it*, and *when*.

h2. Scope
Applies when incident severity is Sev-1 (customer-visible outage or severe degradation) or when IC declares 'comms mode'.

h2. Roles and responsibilities
* Incident Commander (IC): owns message accuracy and cadence; delegates drafting if needed.
* Customer Success Escalation Manager (CSEM): owns direct enterprise outreach and coordination for Dedicated customers.
* Support On-call: monitors inbound tickets, ensures macros match the current incident status.
* SRE On-call: provides technical status, mitigation progress, and ETA confidence level.

h2. Required channels
* Status page: public (when impact is broad or uncertain).
* Email: targeted to affected enterprise contacts when impact is Dedicated-only or customer-specific.
* Slack internal: #incidents plus the incident channel created by incident-bot.

h2. Cadence
* Initial acknowledgement: within 10 minutes of Sev-1 declaration.
* Updates: every 30 minutes while customer impact is ongoing.
* Resolution message: within 15 minutes of mitigation being confirmed.
* Post-incident summary: within 1 business day (public-facing is optional; customer-facing for enterprise is required).

h2. Content requirements (status page + email)
Each update must include:
* What customers are seeing (symptoms)
* Affected surfaces (Hosted API regions, Dedicated clusters, specific model families)
* Current mitigation steps
* ETA statement with confidence level (high/medium/low)
* Workarounds (if any)

Avoid:
* Overly specific internal details (hostnames, precise exploit details)
* Blaming language

h2. Dedicated-specific communication
If impact is isolated to one or more Dedicated clusters:
* CSEM must notify impacted customers directly (email + optional call).
* Include: whether a maintenance action is required (e.g., rolling runtime restart), expected duration, and whether customer traffic will fail over.
* If a hotfix rollout is planned, provide the rollout window and the rollback criteria in customer-friendly terms.

h2. Templates (copy/paste)
*Initial status page message*

We are investigating reports of elevated errors and latency affecting Redwood Inference. We will provide updates every 30 minutes until resolved.

*Update message*

We have identified the issue and are applying mitigation steps. Some requests may continue to see errors or increased latency. Next update in 30 minutes.

*Resolution message*

The issue has been mitigated and service is returning to normal. We are continuing to monitor closely.
