Known issue note: KV-cache pressure mitigation via model fallback (2025-06)

h2. Issue
Under certain prompt distributions (high variance in context length), KV-cache memory pressure can cause abrupt throughput collapse on some A100 pools, leading to queue amplification and increased tail latency.

h2. Symptoms
* Rising GPU memory utilization with frequent allocator churn
* Increasing queue wait time and p95 latency
* Occasional 503 spikes from admission control

h2. Recommended mitigation (temporary)
If you observe KV-cache pressure and cannot immediately reduce context length or reconfigure batching, you may activate *automatic model fallback* to a smaller compatible model for the affected route(s) to maintain availability.

Suggested policy parameters:
* Trigger: queue_wait_ms > 300 OR gpu_mem_utilization > 92%
* Primary: rw-llama-3.1-70b-instruct
* Fallback: rw-llama-3.1-8b-instruct
* Disable once the pool is stable for 30 minutes

h2. Permanent fix
We are rolling out a KV-cache eviction tuning change and improved allocator settings in runtime release rr-2.18.
