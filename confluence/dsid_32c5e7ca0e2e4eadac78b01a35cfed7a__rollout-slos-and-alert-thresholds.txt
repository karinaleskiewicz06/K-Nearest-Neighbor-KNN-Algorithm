Rollout SLOs and Alert Thresholds for Model Version Changes

h1. Purpose
This page defines the minimum SLOs and alert thresholds that MUST be used (or made stricter) in any production rollout plan for a model version change.

h1. Scope
Applies to Hosted API, Dedicated, and Private (unless customer-specific SLOs are stricter).

h1. Required monitoring windows
* Canary decision window: 30 minutes minimum
* Ramp step window: 30 minutes minimum (or 2x the p95 request duration, whichever is larger)
* Full rollout validation window: 24 hours after reaching 100%

h1. Required metrics and thresholds
Rollout plans MUST include a table of pass/fail thresholds for the metrics below, at minimum.

h2. Reliability
* 5xx rate (gateway + router combined)
  * Pass: <= baseline + 0.05% absolute over 30 min
  * Fail: > baseline + 0.20% absolute over 15 min
* Timeout rate
  * Pass: <= baseline + 0.03% absolute over 30 min
  * Fail: > baseline + 0.10% absolute over 15 min
* Admission rejects (saturation/backpressure)
  * Pass: <= 0.5% over 30 min
  * Fail: > 2.0% over 10 min

h2. Latency
* p95 end-to-end latency (non-streaming)
  * Pass: <= +5% vs baseline over 30 min
  * Fail: > +10% vs baseline over 15 min
* Time-to-first-token (streaming)
  * Pass: <= +10% vs baseline over 30 min
  * Fail: > +25% vs baseline over 15 min
* Long-tail latency
  * Fail condition: p99 > 2.5x baseline for 10 min

h2. Capacity/efficiency
* GPU utilization saturation
  * Fail condition: sustained > 92% for 15 min with rising queue time
* Tokens/sec/GPU
  * Pass: >= baseline - 3% over 60 min
  * Fail: < baseline - 8% over 30 min
* Cache hit rate (prefix/KV)
  * Pass: within -5% absolute of baseline over 60 min
  * Fail: within -15% absolute of baseline over 30 min

h2. Quality guardrails (required even if fallback hides errors)
* Internal eval regression delta
  * Pass: >= baseline - 0.3 (normalized score)
  * Fail: < baseline - 0.7
* Customer-reported quality issues
  * Fail condition: 2+ independent customer reports with reproducible prompts within 24 hours

h1. Required alert bundle
During any ramp above 0%, enable the rollout_slo_guard alert bundle scoped to:
* model alias
* version id
* region

The bundle MUST page SRE oncall for any fail threshold and MUST notify #eng-platform and #eng-runtime for elevated error/timeout alerts.

h1. Rollback guidance
A rollout plan MUST map each fail threshold to an explicit action:
* Automatic fallback (router) OR
* Manual rollback (restore prior policy) OR
* Pause and investigate (only allowed for non-customer-impacting signals)

h1. Notes for Dedicated and Private
* Dedicated: use customer-specific SLOs if stricter; include per-customer dashboards.
* Private: if customer has pinned versions, ensure rollback procedures are documented and customer-approved.
