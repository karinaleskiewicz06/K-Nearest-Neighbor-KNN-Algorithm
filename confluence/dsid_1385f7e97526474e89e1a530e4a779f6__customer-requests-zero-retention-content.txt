Customer requests: zero retention of prompt/response content

# Purpose
Track customers/prospects that require zero retention of prompt/response *content* (inputs/outputs) to ensure consistent implementation and contractual language.

This page is limited to Security/Compliance, Support leadership, and Sales leadership due to customer-sensitive requirements.

# Definition
**Zero retention of content** means: prompt/response bodies are not written to logs, traces, support tooling, or storage systems at rest.

**Allowed (typical):** operational metadata such as request id, timestamps, status codes, latency, model id, region, and aggregated token counts needed for billing and reliability.

# Implementation checklist (summary)
1. Confirm requirement scope: content only vs content + metadata.
2. Set org policy flag: **NO_CONTENT_LOGGING** (Support/Platform).
3. Verify tracing redaction at ingest (drop request/response fields).
4. Disable support replay/content viewers for the org.
5. Confirm billing uses token counts without raw content storage.
6. Provide written confirmation using the security addendum template.

# Notes / pitfalls
- Customers often use synonyms: no logging, ephemeral processing, never persisted, no payloads in traces.
- Be careful not to promise no data retained unless explicitly approved; reliability and billing require some metadata.
