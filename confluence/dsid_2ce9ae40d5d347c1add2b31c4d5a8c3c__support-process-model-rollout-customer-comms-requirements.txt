Support Process: Customer Communications Requirements for Model Rollouts

h1. Purpose
This page defines the minimum customer-facing communication requirements for production model version rollouts. Rollout plans MUST include a Customer communications section that follows these rules.

h1. Who must be notified

h2. Always notify
* Any Dedicated customer receiving the new version as default
* Any Private customer (VPC/on-prem) impacted by the change

h2. Notify when material behavior changes
For Hosted API customers, notify when any of the following change materially for a commonly used alias:
* output format or tool-calling behavior
* maximum context/output token limits
* latency or reliability expectations
* safety behaviors (refusals, content filtering)

h1. Timing
* Dedicated: at least 5 business days before the change window, unless the customer agrees otherwise.
* Private: per customer contract; default to 10 business days prior to the maintenance window.
* Hosted: at least 72 hours prior for material behavior changes; otherwise release notes are sufficient.

h1. Required message content
All customer communications MUST include:
* What is changing (model alias and new version id if customer pins versions)
* Why we are changing (benefit statement)
* When (date/time + timezone + duration)
* Impact assessment (best-case and worst-case)
* How to opt out or pin a version
* How to get help (support channel and escalation path)
* Rollback posture (we can roll back during window if thresholds violated)

h1. Channels
* Dedicated/Private: email to customer technical contacts + a support ticket for tracking
* Hosted: release notes; optionally email to top impacted orgs
* If the change triggers an incident or wide impact, use the status page rules

h1. Approvals
Customer communications MUST be reviewed by:
* Customer Success owner for the account
* Product Manager for inference

For Private customers in regulated environments, also require Security review.

h1. Templates (snippets)

h2. Subject line options
* Upcoming update to <model alias> (new version rollout)
* Maintenance window: model version upgrade for Redwood Inference

h2. Opt-out language (required)
If you need to remain on the previous model version, we can pin your deployment to <old version> for at least 30 days. Reply to this email or comment on the support ticket to request pinning.

h1. Tracking and record
The rollout plan MUST link to the customer ticket(s) and note when the communication was sent.
