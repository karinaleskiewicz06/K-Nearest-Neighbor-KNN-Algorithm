Customer Reference Intake & Approval Process

## Purpose
This page defines the operational workflow for requesting and scheduling a customer reference (call, quote, logo, or public case study) as part of Redwood’s Customer Reference Program.

Goals:
- Make it easy for Sales/CS to request references without surprising customers.
- Protect customer relationships through clear load limits and do-not-contact rules.
- Ensure all public-facing usage (logo/quote/metrics/case study) is approved and auditable.
- Standardize tracking in HubSpot and visibility in Slack.

## Scope (what this covers)
This process applies to:
- **Reference calls** (customer speaks with a prospect; may include SEs).
- **Private quotes** (quote used in a sales deck/email that is not publicly distributed).
- **Public assets** (logo, attributed quote, named case study webpage/PDF, blog, webinar).

Out of scope:
- Analyst references (route via Marketing/Comms).
- Partner references (route via Partnerships unless the customer is the primary subject).

## Definitions
- **Reference request:** An internal request to use an existing customer for a call and/or marketing asset.
- **Reference-ready customer:** A customer approved by CS + AE for reference usage within the defined boundaries.
- **Public usage:** Any distribution outside Redwood (website, social, press, public webinars, public PDF downloads, marketplaces).

## Where to request (single intake path)
All requests must be submitted via the **Customer Reference Intake Form** (internal). Do not DM a customer directly with a reference ask without completing intake and receiving approvals.

Primary coordination channel: **#customer-references** (Marketing Ops + CS + Sales)
- Use this channel for visibility, scheduling updates, and escalation.
- Do not share customer-sensitive details; keep deep context in HubSpot notes.

## Intake form fields (required)
Provide enough context to (a) route approvals and (b) prep the customer so the call is successful.

### Requestor + deal context
- Requestor name, team (AE/CSM/SE/PMM)
- Deal name + HubSpot Deal URL
- Deal stage (e.g., Security Review / Procurement / Final Eval)
- Target close date
- Prospect company + industry
- Primary objective for the reference (e.g., Dedicated reliability, Private/VPC compliance, cost-per-token)

### Requested reference type
Select one:
- Reference call
- Private quote (non-public)
- Public quote and/or logo
- Public case study (named customer)
- Webinar/event participation

### Customer + relationship ownership
- Customer company (HubSpot Company URL)
- Account owner (AE) and CSM
- Preferred customer contact(s) (name/title/email) OR “needs intro”
- Relationship health (Green/Yellow/Red) and rationale
- Any active escalations, support concerns, or upcoming renewal (yes/no + notes)

### Proposed scope and boundaries
- What you want the customer to cover (2–4 bullet topics)
- What must be avoided (competitive comparisons, roadmap, pricing, security details, incident history, etc.)
- Any required prospect context (size/region/regulatory constraints)
- Number of calls requested and desired timeframe
- Whether Solutions Engineering support is needed

### Proof/claims (only if quotes/metrics are requested)
- Intended claim(s) (latency, cost, uptime, throughput, deployment model)
- Measurement source (Console dashboard, customer-reported, joint analysis)
- Whether exact metrics or ranges are requested

### Public asset requirements (only if public)
- Asset type (webpage/PDF/blog/webinar)
- Desired publish date
- Required attributions (name/title/company)
- Whether logo usage is requested

## Approval requirements (by request type)
All reference usage requires **CSM + AE approval**. Public usage also requires **Legal approval and executed customer consent**.

### A) Reference call (customer-to-prospect)
Required:
- **CSM approval (required)**: confirms relationship health, contact suitability, and load limits.
- **AE approval (required)**: confirms account strategy alignment and avoids conflicting messaging.

Optional/conditional:
- **Solutions Engineering (recommended)** for technical deep dives (Dedicated sizing, fallback policies, architecture, benchmarks).
- **Legal (not required)** unless the call will include pre-approved public quotes/metrics or the customer requests an NDA-specific addendum.

### B) Private quote (non-public)
Required:
- **CSM approval (required)**
- **AE approval (required)**
- **Marketing/PMM review (required)** if the quote includes performance or security claims.

Legal:
- **Legal review (conditional)** if attribution is included and the customer has restrictions on internal use, or if the quote is derived from materials subject to special terms.

### C) Public quote/logo/case study/webinar
Required:
- **CSM approval (required)**
- **AE approval (required)**
- **Marketing/PMM review (required)** for messaging and claims
- **Legal approval (required)**
- **Executed customer consent (required)** before publication

Reference: follow the restricted Legal guidance in *Customer Logo & Quote Consent Playbook*.

## SLA and lead-time expectations
### Intake response SLA (internal)
- **Within 1 business day:** Marketing Ops acknowledges intake in #customer-references and assigns an owner.
- **Within 2 business days:** CS/AE approvals collected or blockers identified.

### Scheduling SLA (reference calls)
- Target scheduling lead time: **7–10 calendar days** from intake submission to call date.
- If a deal is time-sensitive, mark the request as **“expedite”** with rationale; expedite requests are not guaranteed.

### Customer review window (public assets)
Plan for:
- Draft review: **5 business days** (minimum)
- Legal/brand approval: **10–15 business days** typical for enterprise customers
- Publication buffer: avoid committing to a publish date until legal review is in progress and the customer has acknowledged the timeline.

## Do-not-contact (DNC) rules
We do not ask customers for references when it creates relationship risk or violates prior commitments.

### Hard stops (do not proceed)
- Customer is in a **Red** health state, active escalation, or unresolved Sev-1/Sev-2 incident impact.
- Customer has explicitly opted out of references or is in a **cooldown** period.
- Customer is within **60 days of renewal** or in a sensitive commercial renegotiation, unless the CSM explicitly approves and the customer proactively offers.
- Customer contact has requested no outreach from Sales/Marketing outside agreed channels.

### Soft stops (requires explicit CSM justification)
- Customer has had a major support issue in the last 30 days (even if resolved).
- Customer is mid-migration, mid-audit, or under unusual operational load.
- Customer requires strict confidentiality (e.g., only anonymized public language).

### Reference load limits
Default guardrails (can be stricter per customer):
- **Max reference calls:** 2 per month
- **Cooldown:** 14 days after any reference call before scheduling another (unless customer requests otherwise)
- **Timebox:** 30 minutes standard; 45 minutes only with customer agreement

Marketing Ops + CSM are responsible for enforcing limits using HubSpot tracking fields (see below).

## Operational workflow (step-by-step)
### 1) Submit intake
- Requestor completes the Customer Reference Intake Form.
- Marketing Ops posts a summary in **#customer-references** and links the HubSpot records.

### 2) Triage and routing (Marketing Ops)
Marketing Ops checks:
- Is the requested customer already reference-ready?
- Any DNC flags in HubSpot?
- Does request require Legal/public consent?
- Are there known scheduling constraints (time zones, availability windows)?

### 3) CS + AE approval
- CSM confirms contact, health, load limits, and “avoid list.”
- AE confirms request aligns with account strategy (no surprise asks, no conflicting narratives).

If either declines:
- Marketing Ops closes the request as “Denied” in HubSpot with a short reason.
- Provide alternative customer options if available.

### 4) Prep package (required for all calls)
Marketing Ops (or assigned owner) prepares a short **Reference Call Brief** including:
- Customer context + the story angle to emphasize
- What to avoid
- Target prospect profile
- Suggested questions and proof points
- Logistics + attendee list

### 5) Customer outreach (CS-led by default)
- **CSM owns customer outreach** unless a specific AE relationship warrants AE outreach.
- Use the approved email templates from the outreach sequence doc.
- Do not forward prospect materials to the customer without checking confidentiality requirements.

### 6) Schedule + calendar hygiene
- Create a calendar invite titled: “Redwood Reference Call (Customer ↔ Prospect) – [Prospect Company]”
- Include: agenda, timebox, attendee roles, and the “avoid list” at the bottom of the invite.
- Add a 10-minute internal prep hold 24–48 hours before the call.

### 7) Conduct call + capture outcomes
- Start by confirming what the customer is comfortable sharing.
- Do not record unless the customer explicitly agrees.
- After the call, requestor logs outcomes in HubSpot (see tracking).

### 8) For public assets: draft, validate, approve, publish
- Writer/PMM drafts using the standard case study template.
- Solutions Engineering validates technical accuracy (architecture, routing/fallback, sizing).
- Legal collects executed consent and approves final attribution/claims.
- Marketing publishes to the docs site / website and notifies Sales/CS.

## Tracking expectations (HubSpot + Slack)
### HubSpot: required fields to update
For every reference request, Marketing Ops ensures the following are set/updated on the **Customer Company** record (and linked Deal when applicable):

Customer reference properties:
- Reference status: Not Eligible | Candidate | Approved (Calls Only) | Approved (Public Allowed) | Paused
- Reference types allowed: Calls | Private Quote | Public Quote | Logo | Case Study | Webinar
- Reference topics approved: Dedicated | Private/VPC | Hosted | Reliability/Fallbacks | Cost/Optimize | Security/Compliance
- Metrics disclosure: None | Ranges only | Exact metrics approved
- Max calls per month (default 2)
- Cooldown until (date)
- Last reference date (date)
- Reference owner (CSM) + Sales owner (AE)
- Consent form status: Not required | Drafting | Sent | Executed | Revoked
- Consent form link (URL)
- Notes (short boundaries + avoid list)

Deal tracking (for influence reporting):
- Reference used: Yes/No
- Reference type used: Call | Case Study | Quote
- Reference customer (company name)
- Influence notes (1–2 sentences: what objection it addressed)

### Slack: visibility + handoffs
All requests must have a thread in **#customer-references** containing:
- Intake summary + HubSpot links
- Approval status (CSM/AE/Legal)
- Scheduled date/time (once confirmed)
- Post-call result (Completed/No-show/Rescheduled) + next steps

Do not paste customer redlines, legal terms, or sensitive security details into Slack.

## Common pitfalls (avoid)
- Bypassing the CSM and surprising the customer with a prospect intro.
- Asking for “exact metrics” without confirming measurement method and approval.
- Overbooking a single champion (enforce load limits and cooldowns).
- Treating a reference call like a sales pitch—customers should share their experience in their own words.
