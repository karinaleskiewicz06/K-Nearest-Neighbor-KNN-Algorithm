Postmortem: Scheduler queue backlog impacted streaming starts (2025-12-03)

h2. Summary
On 2025-12-03, streaming requests experienced delayed first-token times due to a scheduler queue backlog. The backlog was amplified by a retry storm from a subset of SDK clients and insufficient backpressure when the GPU queue was saturated.

h2. Customer impact
* Time window: 14:0315:01 PT
* Symptoms: streaming first token delayed (queue wait time), elevated 429s and 5xx from gateway timeouts
* Affected: hosted traffic in us-west; some dedicated pools saw secondary impact due to shared scheduler control-plane load

h2. Detection
* Alert: "scheduler_queue_depth > 1500" and "first_token_p95 > 8s"
* Support tickets referencing "stream never starts"

h2. Root cause
A regression in the scheduler admission controller made it too permissive under high concurrency, allowing queue depth to grow faster than workers could drain.

h2. Resolution
* Rolled back scheduler admission controller to previous build
* Applied temporary rate limit tightening for affected endpoints

h2. Action items
1) Add load-shedding guardrail for queue wait time (Owner: Serving Runtime)
2) SDK guidance update on retry/backoff (Owner: DevEx)
3) Add synthetic streaming canary that triggers on first-token delay (Owner: SRE)
