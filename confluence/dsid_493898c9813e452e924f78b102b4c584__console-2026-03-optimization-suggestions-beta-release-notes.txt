Console Release Notes — 2026.03: Optimization Suggestions (Beta)

## Summary
We are launching a **private beta** of **Optimization Suggestions** in the Redwood Console as part of **Redwood Optimize**. The beta surfaces **actionable batching and caching recommendations** to help customers reduce inference cost while managing latency and reliability risk.

The experience is designed to be conservative and transparent:
- Savings and latency impact are **estimates**, not guarantees.
- Every suggestion includes a **risk level (Low/Medium/High)** with clear rationale and guardrails.
- The UI links directly to the relevant configuration surfaces so teams can apply changes with their existing rollout controls.

---

## What’s new (Beta)
### 1) Suggestions view in Console
A new **Suggestions** page is available in the Console (for enabled orgs) that:
- Lists top optimization opportunities by **route** and **model**
- Shows **estimated savings** (e.g., $/day and %) and **estimated latency deltas** (p50/p95)
- Includes a **risk badge** with explanation (performance/reliability risk; not security risk)
- Supports filtering/sorting (e.g., by savings, risk, confidence, route/model)

### 2) Suggestion details drawer
Selecting a suggestion opens a details drawer with:
- “Why we recommend this” explanation
- Key assumptions and inputs (e.g., request volume, sequence length distribution, cache hit rates)
- Guardrails and when to avoid applying
- Deep links to relevant config pages (batching/caching settings)

### 3) Closed-loop instrumentation
We are instrumenting the beta to learn what is useful:
- Impression → expand → dismiss → apply-click funnel
- Breakdown by org, project, route/model, and region

---

## Scope and limitations (Beta)
This beta is intentionally narrow.

### In scope
- **Batching** suggestions (e.g., configuration adjustments based on observed concurrency/sequence lengths)
- **Caching** suggestions (e.g., enabling/tuning prefix/KV caching behavior when request patterns support it)

### Out of scope (not included in this release)
- Quantization recommendations UI (may be referenced in roadmap, but not launched here)
- Automatic application of suggestions (customers must confirm and apply changes)
- Contractual savings commitments or bill-credit guarantees

### Estimate and risk labeling limitations
- Savings and latency impacts are computed from historical telemetry and modeled assumptions; they may differ from realized results due to workload shifts, routing changes, or traffic seasonality.
- Risk levels reflect **likelihood of latency/reliability regression** when applying the recommendation, given observed traffic patterns and confidence; they do **not** represent security/compliance risk.

### Eligibility notes
- Suggestions require sufficient telemetry history (see below). New projects/routes may show “insufficient telemetry” until enough data is collected.
- In this beta, we prioritize standard Console-managed routes/models. Some custom or experimental configurations may be excluded.

---

## Telemetry requirements
Optimization Suggestions depends on aggregated telemetry and cost attribution signals. To generate recommendations, we require:

### Minimum data signals
- Request volume and concurrency (QPS, in-flight)
- Latency distributions (p50/p95; at minimum enough to compute stable percentiles)
- Token usage/cost attribution by route/model (input/output tokens)
- Sequence length distribution (prompt + completion lengths)
- Cache metrics where applicable (cache enabled state, hit rate/eligible rate, cacheable prefix characteristics)
- Current batching/caching configuration values (to compute a delta)

### Minimum history
- Typically **≥ 7 days** of representative traffic per route/model (some high-traffic routes may qualify sooner; low-traffic routes may require longer)

### Staleness behavior
- Suggestions are recomputed on a scheduled cadence. The UI surfaces a **“stale as of”** timestamp when estimates are based on older telemetry.

## Access and permissions
### Beta access (feature flag)
This release is behind a feature flag and is available only to beta-enabled orgs:
- Feature flag: **optimize_suggestions_beta**

### RBAC
Viewing suggestions requires the appropriate permission:
- Scope: **optimize.suggestions:read** (or equivalent org role mapping)

If a user sees an access denied state, verify role assignment and audit logs per the RBAC runbook.

---

## How to request access (private beta)
To join the beta, customers should:
1) Contact their Redwood CSM, or
2) Open a Support request with subject: **“Optimization Suggestions Beta Access”**

Include:
- Org name / org slug
- Project(s) to enable
- Primary region(s)
- Representative routes/models to evaluate
- Confirmation that telemetry/cost attribution is enabled and collecting

Internal routing note: CSM/Support should follow the enablement steps in **How to enable Suggestions beta feature flag** (linked above) and record the action in the standard audit trail.

---

## Known issues (as of 2026-03)
- Some customers may see “insufficient telemetry” despite high traffic when telemetry ingestion/backfill is delayed. If suspected, check pipeline lag and the Suggestions compute job status.
- A small number of suggestions may show conservative risk levels (Medium/High) even when customers believe their route is tolerant. This is expected when confidence is low or traffic patterns are spiky; we are iterating on confidence calibration during beta.

## Feedback
We are actively collecting beta feedback on:
- Trust and clarity of savings estimates and assumptions
- Risk label comprehension and whether the explanation is actionable
- Missing filters/columns (e.g., by environment, route tags)

Customers can submit feedback via Support. Internally, tag issues with **optimize-suggestions-beta** and include screenshots plus the route/model identifiers and “stale as of” timestamp from the UI.
