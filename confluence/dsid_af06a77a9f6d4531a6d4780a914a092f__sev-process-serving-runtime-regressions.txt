SEV process: Serving Runtime regressions

h1. Scope
This page defines the incident process when a *Serving Runtime regression* impacts latency, throughput, or availability for Hosted or Dedicated inference.

h1. Severity guidelines
* SEV1: widespread customer impact (multi-region Hosted degradation, or multiple Dedicated tenants impacted), or sustained 5xx/timeouts > 2% for >10 minutes.
* SEV2: limited impact (single region, single tenant, or canary cohort) with credible risk of escalation.
* SEV3: low impact or transient regression; monitoring and follow-up required.

h1. Roles
* Incident Commander (IC): runs the incident, owns decisions (including rollback) and comms cadence.
* Operations Lead (optional): executes changes and coordinates engineers doing hands-on work.
* Runtime Lead: technical lead for runtime changes, identifies mitigations and rollback target.
* SRE: monitors SLOs, routing health, and capacity impacts.
* Comms Lead (often CS): owns customer comms and status page updates (per comms guide).

h1. Timeline expectations
* 0-5 min: declare incident (if SEV2/SEV1), create #inc- channel, page runtime+SRE, assign IC.
* 5-15 min: establish symptom, impacted scope, and immediate containment (pause rollout, route away, etc.).
* 15-30 min: decide on rollback vs mitigation if customer-impacting and no fast fix.
* Every 30 min: provide status update to internal stakeholders; for SEV1, update external status per comms guide.

h1. Paging and escalation
* Page: Runtime oncall, SRE oncall, Release captain (if active rollout), and Support lead if customers are opening tickets.
* Escalate to VP Eng if SEV1 lasting > 60 minutes or involving repeated regressions.

h1. Decision record
IC must record in the incident channel:
* Why rollback was chosen (or not chosen)
* The rollback target release tag
* Who approved the action
* Verification results

h1. Wrap-up requirements
Before resolving:
* Confirm metrics stable (Hosted: 30 min; Dedicated: 60 min)
* Ensure comms have been sent (if required)
* Assign follow-ups: root cause analysis, test coverage, rollout guardrails

After resolving:
* File postmortem within 2 business days for SEV1, 5 business days for SEV2
* Link postmortem from the incident channel
